Sourced with quiet respect. Crafted with gentle intention.

At The T Bar, we believe tea is more than a beverage. It is a slow, grounding ritual that connects us back to ourselves and the Earth. Each leaf is sourced directly from heritage family gardens in Uji and Shizuoka, then carefully blended in our serene space.

Step away from the rush. We invite you to sit, hold a warm bowl of matcha in both hands, and listen to the wind drifting through our bamboo courtyard.
