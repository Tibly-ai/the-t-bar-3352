Our Commitment

How We Source and Steep

The path of a single tea leaf from soil to porcelain bowl is long and deliberate. We honor every hand along this journey through four key practices.

Read Our Story
01

Traceable Terroir

We work directly with growers who farm responsibly, utilizing organic pest management and traditional soil cultivation to sustain the mountain air they farm in.

02

Hand-Crafted Processing

From the initial morning leaf plucking to charcoal-fired pan frying and leaf-rolling, our traditional teas are coaxed into shape by hand, never machine shredded.

03

Custom Clay & Water Prep

Water mineral content and temperature are tailored precisely to each varietal, and brewed in porous unglazed clay teapots that balance the tannic profile of our leaves.

04

Mindful Hospitality

Our calm wooden benches, stone floors, and plant-filled tearoom are designed as a quiet space away from screen fatigue, where guests can slow down and focus on taste.
